We have a fully equipped electronics workshop. The following equipment is available:
* soldering station
* SMD soldering station (hot air)
* oscilloscopes
* measuring instruments
* magnifiers
* various tools (pliers, screwdrivers etc.)

There is also a large stock of electronic components, which you can use in exchange for a
donation.

Whether you are soldering your first circuit, getting an Arduino to run or opening up a
broken device to repair it: we are happy to help newcomers to electronics with advice and a
hand.
