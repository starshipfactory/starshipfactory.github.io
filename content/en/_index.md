# Starship Factory - your makerspace in Basel

{{< columns count=2 >}}
{{< column >}}

{{< intro >}}
We are a makerspace, hackerspace, meeting point and open workshop. A colourful mix of creative
makers, artists and geeks from Basel and the surrounding area comes together here. Our members
and guests work on their own and on joint projects, learning, teaching and inspiring each
other.

## What you can do here

{{< columns count=2 >}}
{{< column >}}
{{< img src="/img/home/3d_druck_ecke.jpeg" alt="The 3D printing corner at Starship Factory" sizes="column" >}}

### 3D printing
We have an extensive fleet of 3D printers. You can use them to print your own 3D models
in plastic or resin.

{{< button link="/en/3d-printing/" text="Learn more" style="secondary" >}}
{{< /column >}}
{{< /columns >}}

### Laser
We have a laser engraver and several laser cutters. With our laser cutters you can cut parts
from plywood or acrylic glass, and much more!

{{< button link="/en/laser/" text="Learn more" style="secondary" >}}
{{< /column >}}
{{< /columns >}}

{{< columns count=2 >}}
{{< column >}}
### Electronics
Starship Factory has a very well equipped electronics lab. Here you can solder to your heart's
content, make SMD boards or test your Arduino circuits. We are also happy to help newcomers to
electronics with advice and a hand.
