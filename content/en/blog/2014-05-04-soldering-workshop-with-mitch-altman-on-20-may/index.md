[Mitch Altman](https://noisebridge.net/wiki/User:Maltman23) from the hackerspace
"[Noisebridge](https://noisebridge.net/)" in San Francisco has announced himself as our guest.
On **20 May at 18:00** he will be giving a soldering workshop here at Starship Factory. It is
suitable for beginners who have never soldered and are looking for an easy way in. The kits,
on the other hand, are varied and interesting for the more advanced too.

Mitch brings the soldering kits himself. Among them:

- the [TV-B-Gone](https://cornfieldelectronics.com/tvbgone/tvbg.home.php), for switching off
  any television easily,
- the [Brain Machine](https://learn.adafruit.com/brain-machine), for relaxing and meditating,
- the [Mignonette games console](http://www.mignonette-game.com/), to take along, and
- the [Drawdio](https://learn.adafruit.com/drawdio) audio drawing device

… and [many other kits](https://noisebridge.net/wiki/MitchWorkshopsEU#Kits) to solder
yourself!

Mitch and our competent electronics team are glad to help with guidance and support. You can
find more detailed information in [our flyer](http://wiki.starship-factory.ch/Veranstaltungen/Workshops/MitchAltman/MitchAltmanWorkshopFlyer.pdf)
and on our [wiki information page about Mitch's workshop](http://wiki.starship-factory.ch/Veranstaltungen/Workshops/MitchAltman/).

Never been to Starship Factory? Directions are on our page
"[How to find us](/en/how-to-find-us/)".

Please register in advance and bindingly by email to
[workshops@lists.starship-factory.ch](mailto:workshops@lists.starship-factory.ch). We look
forward to a lively turnout!

If you would like to help us, you are welcome to print and distribute
[our workshop flyer](http://wiki.starship-factory.ch/Veranstaltungen/Workshops/MitchAltman/MitchAltmanWorkshopFlyer.pdf)
yourself.

The workshop was a complete success! Many thanks to all the helpers and especially to the
[Odenwilusenzer](http://www.odenwilusenz.ch/node/351), who lent us their equipment. Without
you we could not have offered so many places, thank you! And of course many thanks to Mitch,
who gave this great workshop. You're awesome!
