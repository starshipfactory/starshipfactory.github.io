On 02 May at 19:00 an electronics workshop takes place at Starship Factory.

This workshop is aimed at electronics beginners looking for their first way into electronics.

Depending on the participants' interests we will cover the following topics:

- Resistance, voltage, current. What are they?
- Simple resistor circuits
- Voltage dividers
- Calculating series resistors, and LEDs
- Simple transistor circuits

The participation fee is CHF 15, one drink included.

The electronic components and accessories are provided on loan.
If you would like to buy them afterwards, CHF 5 of the participation fee is credited.

Registration is [**absolutely required**](mailto:board@lists.starship-factory.ch?subject=Anmeldung%20Elektronik%20Workshop).
