You had to wait a long time, but now the time has finally come: there is another workshop on
**opening locks for beginners** (and the advanced)! In a relaxed atmosphere and with friendly
fellow learners you will learn how a lock is built, how locks can be opened without force, and
what to look out for when buying a lock. A large number of practice locks offers plenty of
challenges. No prior knowledge is needed to take part; everything required is learned on site
during the workshop.

What does that look like?
[Here are some examples from our past workshops.](https://www.flickr.com/photos/julianeclausen/albums/72157652088509521)

It starts on **11 December 2015 at 19:00** [at Starship Factory](/en/how-to-find-us/). If you
[register by email](mailto:workshops@lists.starship-factory.ch?subject=Anmeldung%20zum%20Lockpicking-Workshop)
in good time, you do not need to bring your own pick set. Taking part costs CHF 25 including a
pick set.

**What?** _Workshop "opening locks for beginners" — opening locks without force_
**When?** _11 December 2015 at 19:00_
**Where?** _Starship Factory, Auf dem Wolf 13, Basel_
**How much?** _CHF 25 including pick set_
