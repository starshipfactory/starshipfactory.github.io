On Friday we were given an [Xbox Kinect](http://wiki.starship-factory.ch/Equipment/Xbox_Kinect/)
by one of our members.

That finally gives us the equipment to test
[Reconstruct Me QT](https://code.google.com/p/reconstructme-qt/) as 3D scanning software. So
hopefully we will soon be able to do 3D scans!

Documentation on the [Kinect wiki page](http://wiki.starship-factory.ch/Equipment/Xbox_Kinect/)
has already been started. If our plan works out, there will soon be a guide there on how to
make 3D scans with a Kinect!

But in any case there are certainly plenty of ways to have fun with the device.
