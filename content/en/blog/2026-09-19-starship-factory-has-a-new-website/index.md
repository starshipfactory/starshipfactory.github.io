Starship Factory has a new website.
<!--more-->
We've modernised our website and now build it with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/). [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/)
is a Go framework for generating static websites.

Our website is now trilingual: German, French and English.
Our workshop and club room sit in the Dreiländereck, the corner where Switzerland, France and Germany meet. The new languages are our way of reflecting that.

Along with the redesign, we've retired our outdated wiki and brought all the content back up to date. Is something
missing? We'd love to hear your feedback in our [Signal group](https://signal.group/#CjQKIIt5fkwCXHlImGzm41tTrf-6umAhyM7ENTpqW4Y0P4SHEhDgMyhI63oL7v3mTk0N7G3t).
