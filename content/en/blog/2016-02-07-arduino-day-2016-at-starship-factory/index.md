On **2 April 2016** the international [Arduino Day](https://day.arduino.cc/#/) takes place
again. And this time too we at Starship Factory will be putting on a varied programme. A few
highlights:

- For everyone curious and new to it, Christoph from Starship Factory gives an insight into
  the Arduino world: what is an Arduino? What do you do with it? How can I dive into the
  Arduino world myself, right here?
- Dr. Mathias Wilhelm presents his [HelvePIC32 project](http://www.helvepic32.org/), an
  Arduino-compatible microcontroller to solder together yourself.
- Cedric from CS Audio Design presents his
  [Gesture Based Performance System (GePS)](http://geps.synack.ch/) in a workshop, which turns
  body movements into music.

The detailed programme with all the activities will be published shortly. But do not wait for
it — put **2 April** in your calendar today!

As always we have plenty of Arduino Uno and Arduino Nano boards to tinker with, as well as a
whole shelf wall full of accessories.

If you want to go a step further, you can also design cases for Arduino projects to your own
requirements here and then [3D print](https://wiki.starship-factory.ch/Equipment/3D-Drucker/)
them. We are glad to help with that too. So come by, the visit is worth it! Not just for you,
for your Arduino as well.

So do not forget, and spread the word: **02.04.2016 is Arduino Day**.

**Here is the unofficial timetable for now.**

14:00 to 16:00 introduction to the Arduino world

16:00 to 18:00 HelvePic project

18:00 to 20:00 Gesture Based Performance System workshop

The times may still change, we will keep you posted.

[Everyone curious and interested can register online here](mailto:workshops@lists.starship-factory.ch?subject=Arduinno%20Day%20vom%2002.04.2016)
