Every day you read about new attacks on privacy. Thanks to people like Edward Snowden we know
about the NSA surveillance affair; laptop theft, attacks by spam bots and credit card sharks
are everyday occurrences, and politics keeps pushing for more surveillance and deeper
intrusions into privacy. It is only natural, then, that as a user you want to protect your data
better against other people's access. But what to do?

This September we are offering two workshop days to put an end to that helplessness. Under the
motto "digital self-defence" we show what you as an end user can do for your privacy and that
of your friends and acquaintances. That covers encryption as well as free infrastructure and
anti-censorship measures.

**12 September 2015** is devoted to protecting data in motion. We show how to encrypt email and
how to stop other people being able to tell which pages you open or what you search for.

_15:00 – 15:20:_ keynote
_15:30 – 16:30:_ encrypting email and proving authenticity with GnuPG
_17:30 – 18:30:_ a free network with Freifunk 3ländereck
_19:30 – 20:30:_ anonymous browsing with Tor

**26 September 2015** is more about encrypting data at rest, along with operational security and
secure chat. It also covers what to watch out for if you want to keep your own system and your
data safe and protected from third-party access.

_15:00 – 16:20:_ encrypting storage media with VeraCrypt
_17:00 – 18:00:_ better chatting with off-the-record encryption
_19:00 – 20:20:_ operational security: my system belongs to me!

Taking part in any of the workshops is free of charge.
