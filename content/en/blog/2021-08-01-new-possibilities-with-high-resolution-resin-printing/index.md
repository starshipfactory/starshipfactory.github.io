3D printing has always been a big topic at Starship Factory. Until now, though, you had to make
do with TLD printing. That changed a few days ago: from now on an
[Anycubic Photon Mono X](https://de.anycubic.com/collections/photon-mono-x/products/photon-mono-x-resin-3d-drucker)
MSLA resin printer is available to everyone interested.

The Mono X offers a comparatively large print volume of 192 mm (L) * 120 mm (W) * 250 mm (H) at
an extremely high resolution of 0.05 mm horizontally (4K display) and up to 0.01 mm vertically.
It prints with UV-light-sensitive acrylic resin.

An introduction is required before using the printer. For that you can contact Claudius or
Bernd. At the moment Starship Factory does not yet provide resin. So you would need to get hold
of that yourself before starting to print.

We hope for plenty of interest and look forward to seeing the machine in regular operation
before long.
