The festive season is just around the corner and your Christmas lights have stopped working? Do
not worry! Come to our meetup at Starship Factory and repair your Christmas lights with our
electronics experts.

What can you expect?

* *Professional support*: our experienced techies help you get faulty light chains, stars and
  other illuminated decorations shining again.
* *Tools and materials*: all the tools and materials you need for the repair are available on
  site.
* *A cosy atmosphere*: enjoy a warm and welcoming atmosphere in our workshop, exchange ideas with
  other participants and let the pre-Christmas mood inspire you.

Who can take part? Everyone is welcome, whether you are handy or not. Our electronics experts are
there to help you with advice and hands-on support.

Costs: taking part is free, though we ask for a small donation towards the materials used.

Bring your broken Christmas lights along and give them a new lease of life! We look forward to
seeing you.

Starship Factory team
