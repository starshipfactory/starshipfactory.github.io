**+++ Date changed +++ Date changed +++ Date changed +++**

Unfortunately Starship Factory remains closed to the public because of the coronavirus
ordinance in force. We therefore have to postpone our introduction to 3D printing. It is now
expected to take place on **Friday, 16 April** during our regular opening hours from 20:00.
This is an event by laypeople for laypeople. After a short introduction to the principles of
TLD ("thin layer deposition") 3D printing and the typical workflows, we will explore Starship
Factory's machines together. Depending on how we feel, we will start a series of test prints,
investigate how the machines work, or print first models. After this introduction you will
normally be qualified to use Starship Factory's 3D printers on your own. After that there are
no limits to your creativity.

Please note that the date may shift depending on the pandemic situation.

**Recommended age**: from 14 years

**Prior knowledge needed**: none. Familiarity with 3D model formats is an advantage.

**What you need to bring**: some patience. 3D printing takes a great deal of time.

**Costs per participant**: none. A donation to the 3D printing fund is requested.

**Maximum number of participants**: 4

**Registration**: by email to 3d-druck@rueblitorte.de

**Registration deadline**: none. Registered participants are however given preference.
