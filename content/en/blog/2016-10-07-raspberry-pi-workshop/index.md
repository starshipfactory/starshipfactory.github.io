On 29 October there is another
[Raspberry Pi workshop](https://wiki.starship-factory.ch/Veranstaltungen/Workshops/Raspberry-Pi/)
at Starship Factory.

### Part 1: getting to know the Raspberry Pi

Get to know the Raspberry Pi 3 and build a wireless access point with it.

### Part 2: Raspberry Pi \*blink\*

Learn to control simple electronics and let it become a thing of the internet.

The 2 workshops can be attended independently of one another.

The participation fee is CHF 15, one drink included.

Raspberry Pis and accessories are provided on loan; you only need to bring a laptop.

Anyone who wants to buy one can do so; you will find details at the link above.

Registration is [**absolutely required**](mailto:workshops@lists.starship-factory.ch?subject=Anmeldung%20Raspberry%20Pi%20Workshop).
