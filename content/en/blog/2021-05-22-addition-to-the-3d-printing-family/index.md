We have a new arrival! For everyone who likes it big, an Anycubic
[Chiron 3D printer](https://de.anycubic.com/products/anycubic-chiron-3d-drucker) is available at
Starship Factory with immediate effect. It works by the TLD process and provides a print volume
of up to 400 mm x 400 mm x 450 mm (W x D x H).

The machine has kindly been made available to us by our member and president Pat. After a
general introduction to 3D printing it can be used like Starship Factory's other 3D printers.

The [Cakewalk extension](https://www.lapatisserienumerique.com/en/cakewalk-3d-en/) has also
already arrived and is waiting to be put into service. If you would like to help with
commissioning it, do get in touch with Pat. We are already looking forward to delicious
printing ;-)

We wish you great printing experiences already!
