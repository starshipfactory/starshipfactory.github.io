At today's Starship Factory session we worked on running embedded computers off batteries. The
[OpenVario flight computer](http://www.openvario.org) is powered by the solar cell via a
regulator. The battery serves as a backup and should allow around 18 hours of offline
operation.

On the side we consumed streams from the
[Chaos Communication Congress](https://events.ccc.de/congress/2015/) and
[Mate](http://www.clubmate.de).
