After things had always been a little cramped in the previous 3D printing corner, Claudius took
the initiative and secured us a few metres of high-quality metal shelving. We have now put it up
and moved the printers over, for the most part.

That finally gives us room for relaxed working, and for expansion too. The new printer provided
by Ben, for instance, has found its home there. As a side effect we were also able to finally
store the film rolls for the plotter, and the plotter itself, properly.

The tool benches that previously stood where the shelving is now have been dismantled and stowed
in the shelf in the corridor. The same goes for the tools. We took the opportunity to sort things
out as well. The items sorted out are currently in the wire basket between the shelves, waiting
there to be disposed of.

The redesign of the 3D printing corner also opens up the possibility of rearranging the previous
shelving system and the couch corner. Everyone is invited to start thinking about how the
available space could be divided up and used even better.

We wish everyone a lot of fun using the new possibilities.
