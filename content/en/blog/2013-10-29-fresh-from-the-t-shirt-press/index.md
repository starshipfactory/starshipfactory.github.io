Today we inaugurated the cutting plotter and the T-shirt press. Now you can print T-shirts
here too!

With our plotter, Poli-Flex Premium film needs a contact pressure of 65 g. After that it wants
to be pressed for 20 seconds with the carrier film and another 15 seconds without it, at
165 °C in the T-shirt press. Designs should be saved as vector graphics in SVG format. More
information about T-shirt printing at Starship Factory is in the
[wiki](https://wiki.starship-factory.ch/Equipment/T-Shirt_Druck/).
