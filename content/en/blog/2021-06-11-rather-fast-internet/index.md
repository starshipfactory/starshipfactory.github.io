We have had a fast internet connection for a while. The hardware was not quite there yet. Now we
have upgraded and installed a new router. With it we can finally make full use of the bandwidth
of our internet connection.

We have also installed network sockets on the window side of our new worktable, so that you can
benefit as much as possible from the fast connection.

For everyone whose computer no longer has an Ethernet port, there is fast wifi as an
alternative — 802.11ac with a nominal 1.75 Gbit/s and, in practice, effective transfer rates of
around 300 Mbit/s.
