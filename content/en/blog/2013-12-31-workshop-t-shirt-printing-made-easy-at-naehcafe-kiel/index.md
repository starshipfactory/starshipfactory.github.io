Now that the congress is over, our T-shirt printing experts are making a short stop in Kiel,
where we want to spread our knowledge of T-shirt production. So there will be a workshop on
T-shirt design at the local Nähcafé. There we will turn your T-shirt idea, or one of our
templates, into a finished printed T-shirt which you can take away with you straight after the
course. We will show you the basics of the open source program
[Inkscape](http://inkscape.org/en/) (Mac, Win, Linux) and convert a template (a photo or logo,
say) into a vector graphic suitable as a motif.

While we cut the finished motif together on the cutting plotter, you will learn what to watch
out for when plotting. After that you get an introduction to operating our textile transfer
press — and your T-shirt is done!

- Date: 8 January, 18:00 to 21:00
- Duration: 1 × 3 hrs.
- Fee: EUR 30.- plus material costs for the shirt and flex film (approx. EUR 10.-, depending
  on the motif)
- Bring along: laptop, possibly a template/idea for a motif
- Course leaders: Starship Factory Basel/CH
- Venue: [Nähcafé Kiel](http://naehcafe-kiel.de/)
- No particular prior knowledge is required. If you have questions about the workshop, send an
  email to: [workshops@lists.starship-factory.ch](mailto:workshops@lists.starship-factory.ch?subject=T-Shirts%20Bedrucken%20Leicht%20Gemacht%20Kiel%20Jan%202013)

We take binding registrations by telephone on **0431 / 65 99 67 57** or by email to
[workshops@lists.starship-factory.ch](mailto:workshops@lists.starship-factory.ch?subject=T-Shirts%20Bedrucken%20Leicht%20Gemacht%20Kiel%20Jan%202013).
The course runs from 4 participants. Anyone who already knows how to design T-shirt motifs with
Inkscape can also simply drop in at the Nähcafé during the day on 9 January and have a T-shirt
printed.

You live in Switzerland and are interested in T-shirt printing? From 13.01.2014 we will be
back on site in Basel with our equipment.
