In IT, many tasks consist of managing machines, configuring additional machines and keeping the
current configuration up to date. In the past that was done by hand by so-called sysadmins, who
in reaction to it frequently displayed particular forms of derangement.

Fortunately there are systems these days to automate these tasks and formalise the setup of
systems. This meetup is about ansible, a tool for that purpose.

At the first meeting this evening, **on 24 June 2016 at 19:00** at
[Starship Factory](/en/how-to-find-us/), the idea is that the various users of Ansible and the
people who would like to use it get to know each other first of all and discuss the
possibilities and areas of application of Ansible.
