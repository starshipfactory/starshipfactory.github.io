Dear makers,

Starship Factory invites you on 7 March between 14:00 and 17:00 to an Easter decoration
workshop. We will burn Easter motifs out of thin wooden sheets with the laser cutter and then
colour them in. Anyone who feels like joining in can drop by at any time. There is no fixed
start time.

We have already prepared a range of
[motifs](https://rueblitorte.de/owncloud/index.php/s/x5JBZJfdqC3bpPx). If you want to design
your own, please bring your own laptop. In that case you need a vector drawing program that can
create SVG files (Inkscape, for example). You will find further information in our
[wiki](https://wiki.starship-factory.ch/Equipment/China_Laser/). For a small number of
participants we can provide laptops as well.

For the "finish" of the wooden motifs you are welcome to bring your own paints and other
decorating material. Basic supplies are available. Of course you can also simply leave the
pieces natural.

The workshop is free of charge. Donations are however requested (we will put out a collection
box). If you would like to come, please sign up in the
[Doodle](https://doodle.com/poll/a9taqntmd74qbvvd). That way we can estimate how much material
and how many instructors we need.

We look forward to seeing many of you!

Your Starship Factory
