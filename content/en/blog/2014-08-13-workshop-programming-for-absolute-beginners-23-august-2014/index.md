Have you always wanted to learn to program, but thought until now that it was too hard for
you, or simply never got round to it? We have exactly the right workshop for you, and it is
easier than you think.

Come by and let us convince you! **On Saturday, 23.08.2014 from 13:00 to 19:00**, Alexander
Schremmer (xoraxax) is giving an **introduction to the basics of programming using the
programming language "Python".** Participants must be 16 or older. No prior knowledge of
programming is needed; knowing some English is useful (but not required).

As an introductory exercise we will write programs together that draw national flags. Anyone
who likes is welcome to join us afterwards at the
[Demodays in Olten](http://demodays.org/de/demodays-2014) (20:00). There you can see small
programmed works of art competing against each other.

This workshop is **free of charge**; we are of course glad of a [donation](/en/donate/).

To make planning easier at our end, it would be nice if you could register at
[workshops@lists.starship-factory.ch](mailto:workshops@lists.starship-factory.ch?subject=Programmieren%20f%C3%BCr%20absolute%20AnfaengerInnen%2023.8.14).
People who decide spontaneously are welcome all the same.

**Questions and registrations** to:
[workshops@lists.starship-factory.ch](mailto:workshops@lists.starship-factory.ch?subject=Programmieren%20f%C3%BCr%20absolute%20AnfaengerInnen%2023.8.14)

If possible you should **bring** your own **laptop** running MacOSX, Linux or Windows, on
which you have admin rights to install the software. Please write us an
[email](mailto:workshops@lists.starship-factory.ch?subject=Python%20Workshop%2023.8.14%20kein%20eigener%20Laptop)
if that is not possible.

Never been to Starship Factory? Directions are on our page
"**[How to find us](/en/how-to-find-us/)**".
