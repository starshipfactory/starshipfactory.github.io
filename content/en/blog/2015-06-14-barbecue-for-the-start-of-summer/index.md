On 21 June the time has come again: summer begins! So we would like to invite you warmly to
Starship Factory for a barbecue and shop talk.

When? **21 June 2015, 14:00**

Where? [Auf dem Wolf 13 in Basel](/en/how-to-find-us/)

Please bring along whatever you want to grill yourself. Drinks and snacks are, as always,
already taken care of. Salads and other side dishes are very welcome.
