On Saturday, 2 April 2016, the time has come: Arduino Day is upon us!

A few highlights of the day:

- 16:00: [HelvePic32](http://www.helvepic32.org/): Arduino-compatible microcontrollers to build
  yourself, by Dr. Mathias Wilhelm

- 18:00: [GePS – the gesture-based audio performance system](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2d7getzOCTo)
  by Cedric Spindler

This and more on Saturday, 2 April 2016 from 13:30 at
[Starship Factory](/en/how-to-find-us/), Auf dem Wolf 13, Basel. Anyone who likes is welcome to
bring their Arduino or other microcontroller along and get advice, exhilaration and inspiration
from us. We look forward to seeing you!
