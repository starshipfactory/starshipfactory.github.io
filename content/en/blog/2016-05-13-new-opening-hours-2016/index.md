_Starship Factory's_ opening hours have been slightly revised this week.

From now on we are open for you every **Tuesday and Friday** from **19:30 to 22:00**!

Outside those times only members who have applied for a key have access. So if you want to come
to the _Starship Factory_ outside opening hours, you can write an email
[to our mailing list](https://wiki.starship-factory.ch/Mailingliste/) or a
[message in our chat channel](https://wiki.starship-factory.ch/IRC/) and ask whether someone
happens to have time or is on site anyway.

If the shock has made you forget where to find us: our address is

> Starship Factory
> Auf dem Wolf 13
> 4052 Basel

We have described how to find us on our [directions page](/en/how-to-find-us/).
