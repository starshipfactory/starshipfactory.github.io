Our friends from [stöckerselig](http://stoeckerselig.ch/) warmly invite you to the Weltraum
during Art Basel. It becomes a pop-up gallery once more, with artists from Basel, Switzerland,
Europe and the rest of the world. For visitors it is a platform for exchange and networking,
and a unique chance to see art that will only reach Art Basel in XX years' time.

**35 curating artists/art teams invite 37 artists/art teams!**
The art and networking project during Art Basel, in its second, expanded edition!

**14–16 June 2017 | Auf dem Wolf 13, Basel (CH),** [map of the Weltraum](/en/how-to-find-us/)

**Wednesday 14 June 2017**
\>> from 21:00 – open end | opening party with raclette
\>> from 22:30 Lisbon Trilogy | part I – large-scale projection in the outdoor area of the
Weltraum by Peter Hölscher and Ronald Gaube (Leverkusen), area-composer.de

**Thursday 15 June 2017**
\>> from 19:00 – midnight | bar, food, video and art
\>> 20:00: Susann Wintsch and Linda Herzog talk about their current collaborative projects with
Turkish artists
\>> from 22:30 Lisbon Trilogy | part II – large-scale projection in the outdoor area of the
Weltraum by Peter Hölscher and Ronald Gaube (Leverkusen), area-composer.de

**Friday 16 June 2017**
\>> from 19:00 – midnight | bar, food, video and art
\>> from 22:30 Lisbon Trilogy | part III – large-scale projection in the outdoor area of the
Weltraum by Peter Hölscher and Ronald Gaube (Leverkusen), area-composer.de

By arrangement the rooms are accessible at other times too
