On 10 June from 20:00 a meetup on the subject of fermentation takes place at Starship Factory.
Because everything is transient – and tasty! Here is the description of the event:

Meet like-minded fermentalists, newbies and experienced alike, in the Basel area: everyone is
welcome to join, learn and share cultures, recipes and experiences!

This meetup is for people in the Basel area with an interest in the fermentation process. Come
by, learn from others, and swap recipes, cultures and experiences with the other participants!
Please register by
[email to the workshop team](mailto:workshops@lists.starship-factory.ch?subject=Ich%20m%C3%B6chte%20gerne%20am%20Fermentationstreffen%20teil%20nehmen).
