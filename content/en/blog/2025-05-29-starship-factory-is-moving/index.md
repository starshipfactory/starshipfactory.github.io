Starship Factory is currently closed.

Since we have to vacate our club rooms in the interim use at Klybeck, we looked for suitable
premises and found them on Gasstrasse, near Voltaplatz. At the moment the rooms at Klybeck are
closed as we are in the middle of the move.

From mid-June you will find us (though not yet fully set up) at Gasstrasse 20, in the rear
courtyard.
