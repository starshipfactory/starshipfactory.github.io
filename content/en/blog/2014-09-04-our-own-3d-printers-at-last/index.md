We would like to thank the [E.E. Zunft zu Hausgenossen](http://www.hausgenossen.ch/) for their
generous donation of CHF 10,000 towards our
[3D printers](http://wiki.starship-factory.ch/Equipment/3D-Drucker/).

With the money we bought 6 3D printers of our own for our FabLab, plus material, which our
members and visitors make busy use of to be creative or to produce prototypes, for example.
From the income this generates we can afford repairs to the 3D printers, buy more material and
perhaps later invest in other interesting equipment too.

We are very grateful for this contribution, as our still young association could not have
raised the funds under its own steam. It puts us in a position to meet demand and to win new
members as well.

We are of course also glad of donations from other people, including smaller ones. More about
that [in the donations section of our website](/en/donate/).
