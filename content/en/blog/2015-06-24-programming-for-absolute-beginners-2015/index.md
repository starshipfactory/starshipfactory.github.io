Have you always wanted to learn to program, but thought until now that it was too hard for you,
or simply never got round to it? We have exactly the right workshop for you, and it is easier
than you think.

Come by and let us convince you! **On Saturday, 27.06.2015 from 14:00 to 16:30**, Alexander
Schremmer (xoraxax) is giving an **introduction to the basics of programming using the
programming language "Python".** Anyone who wants to learn to program can take part — member or
not. No prior knowledge of programming is needed; knowing some English is useful (but not
required).

For taking part in this workshop we ask for a contribution towards expenses of **CHF 20**. We
are of course also glad of further [donations](/en/donate/).

To make planning easier at our end, it would be nice if you could register at
[workshops@lists.starship-factory.ch](mailto:workshops@lists.starship-factory.ch?subject=Programmieren%20f%C3%BCr%20absolute%20AnfaengerInnen%2027.6.15).
People who decide spontaneously are welcome all the same.

**Questions and registrations** to:
[workshops@lists.starship-factory.ch](mailto:workshops@lists.starship-factory.ch?subject=Programmieren%20f%C3%BCr%20absolute%20AnfaengerInnen%2027.6.15)

If possible you should **bring** your own **laptop** running MacOS X, Linux or Windows, on which
you have admin rights to install the software. Please write us an
[email](mailto:workshops@lists.starship-factory.ch?subject=Python%20Workshop%2027.6.15%20kein%20eigener%20Laptop)
if that is not possible — there are a few visitor laptops available.

Never been to Starship Factory? Directions are on our page
"**[How to find us](/en/how-to-find-us/)**".

We look forward to meeting you on Saturday! Your Starship Factory team.
