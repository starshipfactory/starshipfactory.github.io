Dear Starshiponauts and interested people!

On **Friday, 27.03.2015 at 19:30**, Flint will give a talk about keeping ball pythons at
Starship Factory (see the [directions](/en/how-to-find-us/)). The talk is open to
non-members too and lasts about 40 minutes. Questions can be asked afterwards.

The speaker would like to introduce interested people to this particular hobby, or rather pet.
He owns four ball pythons himself. In this talk he covers facts about this pet as well as
characteristics you have to pay attention to if you want to keep a ball python yourself.

See you soon at Starship Factory!
