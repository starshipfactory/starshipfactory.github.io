On **18 May 2016** a [UEFA Champions League football match](http://www.fussball.ch/Sevilla+und+Liverpool+kommen+nach+Basel/696079/detail.htm)
takes place at the St. Jakob stadium. As a security measure the entire area around the St.
Jakob stadium will be sealed off by the police.

Unfortunately that affects our club rooms too. So on **18 May** it will presumably not be
possible for members and visitors, with or without a key, to use or enter Starship Factory's
rooms.

We are sorry for the inconvenience and will open our door as usual again on _20 May at 19:30_.
