On Sunday, 26 June 2022 from <b>11:00</b> we are holding the Klybeck Arealfest. We and many
others will open our doors and give an insight into what we do.
There are many small projects for young and old to try out.

With us you can try out smaller projects with the 3D printer, laser cutter and cutting plotter.
In the Makey Makey workshop you will learn to build a banana controller, for your favourite game
for instance (bring your phone), and to get to know the tinkerer in you.
