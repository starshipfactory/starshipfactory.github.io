Dear makers, and everyone who still wants to become one: we are open again! And have been since
25 May. So you are all welcome again on our open days. These are, as before:

- Tuesdays from 19:30 to 22:00
- Fridays from 19:30 to 22:00

and by arrangement on other days too, of course.

Because of the coronavirus pandemic our protection concept currently applies. We ask you to take
note of it below and to put it into practice on site, for everyone's benefit.

**Hand hygiene**

- Clean and/or disinfect your hands when entering the premises.
- Clean and/or disinfect your hands when changing tools and workplace.
- Clean and/or disinfect your hands when leaving the premises.

**Keeping your distance**

- No more than one person per workplace.
- At the plenary or in meetings, observe the 2-metre distancing rules or attend online instead.
- The total floor area allows 7 people in the Starship Factory rooms, 10 in the rest of the
  forum.

**Work with unavoidable distance under 2 metres**

- For work requiring instruction, at least a mouth-and-nose covering is recommended.

**Cleaning**

- Tools and machines must be cleaned after use.
- Use cleaning agents permitted by the machine's operating instructions.

**Particularly vulnerable people**

- Particularly vulnerable people are advised to stay at home.

**People ill with Covid-19 at the workplace**

- Ill people stay at home.
- Should an ill person be identified at the SF, they will be sent home immediately with a
  protective mask, for the protection of others.

We look forward to seeing you!

Your Starship Factory
