Oops, is it the end of the year again? Only one thing helps: change down a gear, roll to a stop
and switch into contemplative mode.
An excellent opportunity to sit together again, look back over the year and do a bit of lasering
(what else)!
This year we would like to build snowflakes with you, since snow has become rare in the Rhine
plain. We defy global warming with
[Bleeptrack's snowflake generator](https://fr0zensystem.bleeptrack.de/) and create our own
diversity that way.
Of course you are welcome to laser other things too. If you are artistically gifted you have the
chance to pick up a pen and create your own cutting templates with our help.
The materials for the workshop are provided. There is also non-alcoholic punch and, if you
prefer, mulled wine — plus harmful carbohydrates for everyone too weak to resist.
We are already looking forward to seeing you!

**Date and time**: 03.12.2023 (Sunday) from 10:00

**Location**: Starship Factory, building K-102, Klybeckstrasse 141, 4057 Basel
([directions](/en/how-to-find-us/))

**Recommended age**: from 12 years, or younger accompanied by an adult

**Prior knowledge needed**: none

**What you need to bring**: good spirits

**Costs**: none. We would be glad of a donation towards materials and machine use.

**Registration**: no registration is necessary. You are welcome to drop by spontaneously.
