This Saturday, 28 March 2015, is international [Arduino Day](https://day.arduino.cc/#/)!

From 14:00 to 18:00 you can bring your own Arduino projects
[to us at Starship Factory](/en/how-to-find-us/), talk about microcontrollers, or find out
what an Arduino actually is. There is bound to be an exciting new project in it for someone.

Do you not have an Arduino of your own, or are all your Arduinos already tied up in other
projects? No problem: Arduinos and other microcontrollers are available here at Starship
Factory too, for a contribution towards costs.

Have you not been here before? You will find our new rooms under
[How to find us](/en/how-to-find-us/) on our website.
