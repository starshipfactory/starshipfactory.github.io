In August we acquired an Anycubic Photon Mono X resin printer to extend our possibilities in 3D
printing. To go with this printer there is now also a
[Wash and Cure 2.0 unit](https://www.anycubic.com/collections/wash-cure-machine/products/wash-cure-machine-2-0).
It makes washing the printed parts considerably easier. Soft and sticky surfaces are a thing of
the past.

Do come by and try it out. As with the Mono X, an introduction is required before first use. For
that you can contact Claudius or Daniel.
