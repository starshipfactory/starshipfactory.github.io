On 14 April the Federal Council decided on relaxations. From the week of 19 April, Starship
Factory is therefore finally opening its doors to the public again. From that date you are
welcome again during regular opening hours. These are, as before:

- Tuesdays from 19:30 to 22:00
- Fridays from 19:30 to 22:00

and by arrangement on other days too, of course.

Our established protection concept continues to apply. We ask you to take note of it below and
to put it into practice on site, for everyone's benefit.

**State of health**

- Please only come to Starship Factory if you feel 100% healthy.
- This applies in particular if you notice possible symptoms of a COVID illness (fever, dry
  cough, loss of smell, for example).

**Mouth and nose protection**

- A mouth and nose covering must be worn throughout your stay at Starship Factory.

**Keeping your distance**

- For workplaces: no more than one person.
- When working in a group, the 2-metre distancing rule must be observed.

**Hand hygiene**

- Clean and/or disinfect your hands when entering and leaving the premises.
- Clean and/or disinfect your hands when changing tools or workplace.

**Cleaning**

- Tools and machines must be cleaned after use.
- When cleaning, use only cleaning agents permitted by the operating instructions.

**Particularly vulnerable people**

- Particularly vulnerable people are advised to stay at home.

Stay healthy and hopefully see you soon. We look forward to seeing you!

Your Starship Factory
