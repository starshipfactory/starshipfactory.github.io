Round two: on 2 July from 20:00, Claudius and Bernd are offering our uncomplicated and entirely
informal introduction to 3D printing at Starship Factory once again.

This is an event by laypeople for laypeople. After a short introduction to the principles of TLD
("thin layer deposition") 3D printing and the typical workflows, we will explore Starship
Factory's machines together. Depending on how we feel, we will start a series of test prints,
investigate how the machines work, or print first models.

After this introduction you will normally be qualified to use Starship Factory's 3D printers on
your own. After that there are no limits to your creativity. We are already looking forward to
seeing you!

**Recommended age**: from 14 years

**Prior knowledge needed**: none. Familiarity with 3D model formats is an advantage.

**What you need to bring**: some patience. 3D printing takes a great deal of time.

**Costs per participant**: none. A donation to the 3D printing fund is requested.

**Maximum number of participants**: 4

**Registration**: by email to 3d-druck@rueblitorte.de

**Registration deadline**: none. Registered participants are however given preference.
