Fresh from the Ultimaker: a multi coloured [full body iPhone4 case](http://www.thingiverse.com/thing:42648)
from Thingiverse.

Because the different colours have different material properties, the black-and-white part
unfortunately turned out a little unstable. The GCode file's temperatures were tuned for the
main colour, turquoise.
