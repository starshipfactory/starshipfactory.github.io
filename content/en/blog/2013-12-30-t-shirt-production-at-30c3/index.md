Until tomorrow we are present at the
[30th Chaos Communication Congress in Hamburg](https://events.ccc.de/congress/2013/wiki/Main_Page)
with an [assembly](https://events.ccc.de/congress/2013/wiki/Assembly). Thanks to numerous
interested visitors, the [cutting plotter](http://wiki.starship-factory.ch/Equipment/Schneidplotter/)
and the [T-shirt press](http://wiki.starship-factory.ch/Equipment/T-Shirt-Presse/) are running
almost continuously, and people are busily developing, plotting and pressing their own T-shirt
designs.

The important part of the process is less the T-shirt itself than that visitors learn
thoroughly how to print T-shirts themselves under guidance — from the graphic to the press.

![Designing a motif in Inkscape](/img/blog/motiv-design-in-inkscape_2.jpeg "Designing a motif in Inkscape")

Unfortunately we do not yet have motifs of our own in stock, but even so our visitors find
plenty of designs they have always wanted to make themselves.

![A proud owner of a new, self-made FreeBSD T-shirt](/img/blog/ein-stolzer-besitzer-eines-neuen-selbst-gemachten-freebsd-t-shirts_2.jpeg "A proud owner of a new, self-made FreeBSD T-shirt")

But a great many interested people also put real effort into designing their own motifs. Often
enough the T-shirt is a present for friends, acquaintances or the children:

![A Tigerente for the younger ones](/img/blog/eine-tigerente-fur-jungere_2.jpeg "A Tigerente for the younger ones")

![Lena's children's T-shirt](/img/blog/lenas-kinder-t-shirt_2.jpeg "Lena's children's T-shirt")

And some get creative in order to finally treat themselves to a T-shirt of their favourite
project, which simply never had one:

![A visitor wearing a self-printed “Play Life” T-shirt](/img/blog/image_cachef79c5965d31aac2abbf28ba98c5687be_2.jpeg "A visitor wearing a self-printed “Play Life” T-shirt")
