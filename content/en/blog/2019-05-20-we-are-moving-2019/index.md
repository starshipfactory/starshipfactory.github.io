On 30.05.2019 we will be
[moving the entire makerspace](https://wiki.starship-factory.ch/Projekte/Zuegeltermin_Auffahrt_2019/).
That is the Ascension Day public holiday.

We start at the current location on dem Wolf:

- packing all smaller items into crates and boxes
- carrying the crates/boxes down to the basement and stacking them there for removal
- the trailer will probably not arrive on dem Wolf until around 10:00

On 31.05.2019 we are aiming for the following tasks on dem Wolf:

- clean-up operation "Auf dem Wolf"
- handing the keys over to the landlord

We would be glad of helping hands!
