Do you have a unique design that you would like to put onto different materials such as wood or
metal? At Starship Factory we offer you the chance to engrave your design onto a range of
surfaces with state-of-the-art laser technology.

Date: Friday, 29 November 2024
Time: 19:30 – 22:00
Location: Makerspace Basel, Starship Factory, Klybeckstrasse 141, Basel

What can you expect?

* *Introduction to laser technology*: our experts give you an accessible introduction to how
  laser cutting and engraving work and what they can do.
* *Preparing your design*: learn how to prepare your design optimally for the laser process. We
  help you digitise and adapt your design.
* *Hands-on workshop*: under the guidance of our experienced technicians you laser your design
  onto a material, such as a metal sign. You are led through the process step by step and can
  learn all the necessary techniques along the way.

Who can take part? The course is open to everyone, whatever your prior knowledge. Whether you
are an experienced maker or working with laser technology for the first time — we look forward
to seeing you!

Costs: donations are welcome 😀

Registration: no registration is necessary; you can drop by spontaneously.

Contact: for further information and questions you can reach us by telephone on 076 409 57 02.

Do not miss the chance to put your own design onto different materials and get to know the
fascinating world of laser technology along the way. We look forward to your creative ideas and
an exciting workshop!
