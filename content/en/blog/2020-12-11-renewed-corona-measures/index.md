Unfortunately, because of the measures decided today by the Federal Council, we have to restrict
our public opening hours. For that reason Starship Factory is no longer accessible to
non-members with immediate effect.

All members please continue to observe the
[protection concept](/en/2020/06/19/we-are-open-again/) announced in June and displayed on site,
and avoid contact with other people where possible.

We hope to be able to welcome everyone interested back to Starship Factory soon, and look
forward to realising crazy ideas with you.

Stay healthy and see you soon.
