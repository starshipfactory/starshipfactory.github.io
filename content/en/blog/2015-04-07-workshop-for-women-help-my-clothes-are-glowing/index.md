On **Sunday, 19 April 2015** from **14:00 to 18:00** there is a workshop **by women for
women**:

Do you find ordinary clothes too boring too? With EL wire you can add glowing messages in many
different luminous colours! And with our T-shirt film you can put a non-glowing message
underneath.

Please bring the clothes you want to beautify (a hoodie, shoes or jeans, for example) from
home. [EL wire](https://learn.adafruit.com/el-wire/overview), T-shirt films and tools are
provided.

No prior knowledge is required. We will practise working with soldering irons, pliers, sewing
machines and hand sewing needles, hot glue and the like.

Costs: material costs come to about **CHF 20.00** per person. The workshop itself is free.

Have you not been to Starship Factory before? You will find directions under
[How to find us](/en/how-to-find-us/).

Please register by [email](mailto:workshops@lists.starship-factory.ch?subject=Frauenworkshop%2019.4.2015).
