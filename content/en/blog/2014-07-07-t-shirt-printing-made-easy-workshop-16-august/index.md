On **16.08.2014** at **15:00** we are running a **T-shirt printing workshop**.

### With laptop, cutting plotter and transfer press to the design you want on your T-shirt.

Anyone who can use a keyboard and mouse can print T-shirts too. We will show you how! Together
we will turn your T-shirt idea, or one of our templates, into a finished printed T-shirt which
you can take away straight after the course.

Questions and registrations to:
[workshops@lists.starship-factory.ch](mailto:workshops@lists.starship-factory.ch?subject=T-Shirtworkshop%2016.08.2014)

![Example T-shirts](/img/blog/beispiel-t-shirts.jpg "Example T-shirts")

### Course content

In this 4-hour workshop we will show you the basics of the open source program
[Inkscape](http://inkscape.org/) and convert a template (a photo or a logo, say) into a vector
graphic suitable as a T-shirt design.

While we cut the finished design together on the cutting plotter, you will learn what to watch
out for when plotting.

After you have weeded your design, you get an introduction to operating our textile transfer
press — and your T-shirt is finished, printed with the design you wanted.
