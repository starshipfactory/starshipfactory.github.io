On 09 February 2019 at 16:00 (Auf dem Wolf 13, 4052 Basel) this year's regular general assembly
of Starship Factory takes place. The agenda will be sent out in good time (2 weeks before the
assembly). Agenda items can still be submitted until 05.02.2019 to
[board@lists.starship-factory.ch](mailto:board@lists.starship-factory.ch).

Members of Starship Factory are warmly invited; interested non-members are welcome to attend
the assembly but have no voting rights.
