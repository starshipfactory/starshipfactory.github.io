Do you share our enthusiasm for lasers? Then this introduction to working with materials on our
KH-3020 laser might be just the thing for you. It takes place on **Friday, 19 November** during
our regular opening hours from 20:00.

The KH-3020 is a variant of the popular K40 CO2 laser. The controller has however been replaced
to extend what it can do. It is driven with the software LightBurn.

During the introduction we will optimise the cutting and engraving settings for a typical
material together and create a material test card. In doing so we get to know the machine and
its software. After this introduction you will normally be qualified to use Starship Factory's
laser cutter on your own. After that you can let loose with your own projects.

**Recommended age**: from 14 years

**Prior knowledge needed**: none. Experience with vector graphics is an advantage.

**What you need to bring**: some patience. We only have one machine and the engravings in
particular take time.

**Costs per participant**: none. A donation to the laser fund is requested.

**Maximum number of participants**: 4

**Registration**: by email to laserschnitt@rueblitorte.de

**Registration deadline**: none. Registered participants are however given preference.
