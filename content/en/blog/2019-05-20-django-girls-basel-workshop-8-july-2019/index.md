On 8 July the [Django Girls](https://djangogirls.org/basel) will be giving a free workshop at
the [University of Applied Sciences Northwestern Switzerland in Muttenz](https://www.fhnw.ch/de/die-fhnw/standorte/muttenz)
on getting started with building websites using Django. The workshop is open to women and
gender minorities and requires no prior knowledge whatsoever.

Here is a short overview again:

- **When?** 8 July 2019, roughly 9:00 to 19:00
- **Where?** FHNW Campus Muttenz
- **For whom?** Women and gender minorities with no prior knowledge
- **Web:** [https://djangogirls.org/basel/](https://djangogirls.org/basel/)
- **Twitter:** [@DjangoGirlsBSL](https://twitter.com/DjangoGirlsBSL)
- **Instagram:** [DjangoGirlsBasel](https://instagram.com/DjangoGirlsBasel)
