On 29 June a crash course on the Raspberry Pi takes place at Starship Factory. We will show you
what a Raspberry Pi is, how to hold it properly, what power it eats and what entertaining
things you can do with one kept in species-appropriate conditions.

Raspberry Pis and accessories are provided, and using examples we will learn how to carry out
projects like the ones in this great online tutorial yourself, at home or here in the space.

Taking part, including a Raspberry Pi, costs about CHF 65 — depending on the accessories
chosen. Registration is **absolutely required**. Unfortunately the workshop is however
**already fully booked** — further details about the next workshop are on the workshop page.

This workshop is a collaboration with the
[Macintosh Users Switzerland](http://www.mus.ch/component/jcalpro/149-default/150-lt-basel/557-lt-basel?Itemid=86).
