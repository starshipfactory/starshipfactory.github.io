After a year and a half and several attempts, Starship Factory has finally moved into a new
home. Instead of St. Alban-Rheinweg, we have now arrived at our new and own clubhouse at Auf
dem Wolf 13, between the St. Jakob stadium and the Dreispitz suburban railway station.

From now on all regular meetings will take place at our new location, starting with the Tuesday
meet-up on 3 March. We have of course also updated the
[directions](/en/how-to-find-us/)!

We are very glad to have finally found a space of our own, available to us without restriction
at any time of day or night. Our need for space has also grown enormously over the past few
years. It was time to find our own, larger room. As soon as we have finished settling in at the
new location we will of course hold an opening party.

We look forward to plenty of visitors at the new location!

See you soon,
your Starship Factory team
