## By public transport from Basel SBB

_15 minutes from Basel SBB_

Take tram number 1 or 11 towards Dreirosenbrücke and get off at the "Voltaplatz" stop.
{{< /column >}}
{{< column >}}
## By car

_5 minutes (1.8 km) from the Basel St. Johann motorway exit_

Starship Factory is quick to reach by car too.

You will find us at Gasstrasse 20. There is no visitor parking, so please find a space in the
neighbourhood.

Basel's blue parking spaces are free to use Monday to Saturday between 19:00 and 08:00.
Outside those hours you need to
[put a parking disc in the car, set to the next half hour](http://www.polizei.bs.ch/verkehr/strassenverkehr/parkieren.html)
(German), which then lets you park for one hour.
