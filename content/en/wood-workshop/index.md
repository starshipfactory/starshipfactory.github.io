We have a fully equipped wood workshop. From cutting to planing to drilling and assembly, a
piece of furniture, an enclosure or a repair can be taken from start to finish here.

{{< columns count=2 >}}
{{< column >}}
## Table saw and planer
The table saw handles straight cuts in boards and sheets; the planer brings rough timber to
an even thickness and a clean surface.

## Band saw
Use the band saw for curves and rounded shapes a table saw is too straight for — and for
cutting thicker pieces.
{{< /column >}}
{{< /columns >}}

## Wood lathe
Round parts come off the wood lathe: bowls, handles, legs, candlesticks. The wood turns and
you guide the tool by hand.

## Mitre saw
The mitre saw cuts battens and strips to length, including mitres — the quickest way to a lot
of pieces the same length.
{{< /column >}}
{{< /columns >}}

{{< columns count=2 >}}
{{< column >}}
## Pillar drills
At the pillar drills you drill square to the surface and to a consistent depth — more
accurately than a hand drill allows.
