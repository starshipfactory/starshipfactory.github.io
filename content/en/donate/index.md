Starship Factory is of course enormously pleased about donations of any kind. That includes
donated equipment and loans as well as financial support.

## Donations in kind

Practical suggestions for things we could immediately use are on the
[wish list in the wiki](https://wiki.starship-factory.ch/Wunschliste/). We are naturally open
to other donations too — but in that case it is better to write to the
[open mailing list](https://wiki.starship-factory.ch/Mailingliste/) first, so we can make sure
we have room for it.

## Loans

We are also glad to receive machines on loan that we can use within the purposes of the
association. The usage fees they earn are then split to finance the maintenance of the
machine. The usual terms are on the
[usage fees page in the wiki](https://wiki.starship-factory.ch/Vereinskram/Finanzplanung/nutzungsgebuehren/),
but other arrangements can be negotiated.

## Financial support

There are two ways to support the association financially. You can make a donation earmarked
for a specific purpose through
[our crowdfunding mechanism](https://wiki.starship-factory.ch/Wunschliste/Krautfanding/); the
[crowdfunding wiki page](https://wiki.starship-factory.ch/Wunschliste/Krautfanding/) describes
exactly how. In principle it is simply a transfer with a particular reference text to a postal
account.

You can also support us independently of any project. For that, a transfer to the following
postal account is enough:

IBAN: CH15 0900 0000 6073 8720 1\
BIC: POFICHBEXXX\
Clearing no.: 9000

Recipient:

Starship Factory\

Not a fan of bank transfers? No problem! Bring your donation along on your next visit and put
it in the donation box, or give it to
any board member you like.

If you want to support us financially on a regular basis, you are also very welcome to simply
[join the association](/en/become-a-member/) or write an
[email to the board](mailto:board@starship-factory.ch?subject=I%20would%20like%20to%20become%20a%20patron%20of%20Starship%20Factory)
to be taken on as a patron.
