Starship Factory is organised as a *Verein* — a Swiss registered association — so that you
and every other Starshiponaut can realise projects affordably.

Besides cheaper [machine usage and reduced material prices](https://wiki.starship-factory.ch/Vereinskram/Preise/),
members of Starship Factory have the right to
[help decide](https://wiki.starship-factory.ch/Vereinskram/Sitzungen) and shape the flight
path of the project.

The monthly membership fees pay for tools and materials, keep the infrastructure running and
cover the rent. You can become a member from CHF 24.- a month. If you like what we do, we are
very happy about a higher contribution too.

Of course you can also come and make things without being a member. Our
[equipment](https://wiki.starship-factory.ch/Equipment/) is available to you as a guest! And
as a patron you can [support us](/en/donate/) without taking an active part.

Our [most important principles](/en/charter/) are transparency and
[democracy](https://wiki.starship-factory.ch/Vereinskram/Sitzungen/). We want to disclose
everything that happens at Starship Factory, and to document and explain what we build, so
that others can learn from our experience and our mistakes, join in when they feel like it,
and support us with what they know. Nothing should happen in obscurity: it matters that
anyone who wants to know about something can inform themselves, get involved, and take part
in decisions [by direct democracy](/en/statutes/).

Mostly, though, we are a group of people who enjoy making things. The organisational
structure is only there to give us a lasting framework, so we can get on undisturbed with
creating something new and reaching for the stars — maybe one day even with a spaceship!
