3D printing is one of Starship Factory's main focuses. We have an extensive fleet of 3D
printers — from filament printers for sturdy parts to a resin printer for fine detail. Bring
your model along as an STL and slice it on site.

{{< columns count=2 >}}
{{< column >}}
## Prusa XL
The Prusa XL can print with two filaments at once — for two-colour parts, or for support
structures in a different material. It is also our printer for large workpieces.

## Anycubic Photon Mono M7 Max
The Anycubic Photon Mono M7 Max is a resin printer: it cures liquid resin layer by layer with
light. That gives considerably finer detail than a filament printer — ideal for figures,
model making and small, accurate parts.
{{< /column >}}
{{< /columns >}}

{{< columns count=2 >}}
{{< column >}}
## Resin curing station
Our resin curing station for the Anycubic Photon Mono M7 Max. Freshly printed resin parts are
washed and post-cured in it — the step that turns a sticky blank into a finished part.

## Prusa MK4
The Prusa MK4 is our workhorse for everyday prints in PLA and PETG: reliable, quick to set up
and a good first printer if you have never printed before.
